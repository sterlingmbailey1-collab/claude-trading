- **Best vs SPY:** Momentum Breakout (+2.0%) had only 5 hits, too few to judge. Among scans with a real sample, the best was Heavy Put Buying (61 hits, +1.6% vs SPY, 56% right direction). It slightly lagged SMH (−0.4%), so most of that edge came from chip/AI names falling with their sector (NBIS was its biggest winner), not from picking individual stocks.
- **Worst vs SPY:** Low-Float Squeeze (34 hits, −2.4%, 32% win rate, median −2.2%), Gap Up (15 hits, too small a sample; −2.0%, median −4.5%) and Unusual Options Activity (−1.9%). Heavy Call Buying also went the wrong way: −1.3% vs SPY with a 42% win rate.
- **The big bullish scans were index-like.** Strong Uptrend, Quality Value, High-Margin Leaders and Weekly Trend Leaders (300–600 hits each) averaged about 0% and beat SPY by only +0.2–0.4%. They beat SMH by about +2.2–2.4% only because chips fell about 2% this week, which isn't stock-picking skill.
- **Confluence vs single scans:** the 108 confluence picks averaged +0.0% direction-adjusted with 57% in the right direction. That's about the same as the large single bullish scans (≈0%, 51–58% win rates), so confluence added no edge this week. Its three worst picks (NBIS, DJT, SNDK) each fell 8–11%.
- **Thresholds worth testing:** Gap Up, require the gap to hold (close above the open), since the median hit faded 4.5%. Low-Float Squeeze, test a higher minimum price or relative-volume floor, since two-thirds of hits fell.

Screening data only, not investment advice. One week is a small sample, so judge scans over several weeks.
