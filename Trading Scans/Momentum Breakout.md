# Momentum Breakout

Part of [[Robinhood Scans Index]].

**Category:** Momentum · **Bias:** Bullish · **Timeframe:** Daily

## Filters
- % Change (1d) > 3%
- Relative volume (30d) > 2×
- Market cap > $2B
- Last price > $5
- Asset type = Stock

## What it finds
Mid and large caps moving up today on at least double their normal volume.

## How to trade it
Enter on a pullback that holds above the breakout level or VWAP. Stop under the day's low.

## How the bot should use it
